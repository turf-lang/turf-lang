TURF E111 -- NESTED TOO DEEPLY:

CAUSE:
  The parser follows brackets inside brackets, and stops at a fixed depth because
  unbounded nesting can crash a compiler. Reached by hundreds of nested parentheses,
  repeated `!` or `-` prefixes, or deeply nested generic types. Real programs never
  get near the limit, so this almost always means a character was typed over and over
  by accident, or a closing bracket is missing so the depth keeps growing.

INCORRECT:
    mut x: int = ((((((((((((... hundreds ...))))))))))));
    mut y: bool = !!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!...;
    mut z: List<List<List<List<...>>>> ...;

FIX:
  Look at the reported line for a repeated character and delete the extras. For truly
  deep data, name the inner pieces with `mut` and build the result step by step.

NOTE: a registered cascade -- the one nesting mistake may also print a follow-up error.

RELATED: E104 (unclosed delimiter).
